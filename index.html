Course Projects

Choose Your Path: Two Distinct Approaches to Machine Learning Mastery

💡 Course Philosophy

"Enable students to do cool things with machine learning."

Track 1: Structured Learning Path

Two Connected Projects

📊 Midterm Project (10 pts)

"Get your hands dirty with a dataset. Apply what you've learned in class with shallow models."

🎯 Final Project (30 pts)

"Go deeper. Build upon your midterm work with neural networks and advanced techniques."

  • Total: 40 points (10 + 30)
  • Best for: Students who prefer guided structure
  • Timeline: Progressive complexity with clear milestones

Track 2: Independent Research

Single Comprehensive Project

🔬 Research Project (40 pts)

Tackle a bigger, semi-open problem — exploratory, with either a research bent or applied AI innovation.

  • Total: 40 points (single project)
  • Best for: Self-motivated students seeking research experience
  • Timeline: Full semester for in-depth exploration
  • Expectations: Higher standards, more independent work
