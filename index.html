The work

Two kinds of assignment, alternating

One deliverable a week. They trade off, and the pairing is the point — you derive a result on paper, then implement the thing you just derived.

Problem sets

Derive it

Small, hand-worked problems: a derivation, an algorithm traced on numbers small enough for paper, and one question you answer in plain words with no equations allowed.

Coding assignments

Build it

Jupyter notebooks implementing the week’s result from scratch before reaching for a library — so you know what the library is doing when you finally use it.

The project

A semester of research

Everyone runs an independent research project this term. Replicate a paper, extend a method, or take on something genuinely unsolved — then write it up like a paper and give the talk.

Due end of week 2

Proposal

Two to three pages: the question, the prior work you are building on, and a ten-week plan with real milestones. Submitting it early means you spend the term building, not deciding.

Week 6

Midterm checkpoint

Progress against your own plan. What works, what changed, what is left.

Weeks 14–15

Report and talk

A 6–12 page paper-style report and an 8–10 minute presentation. Pairs welcome; small groups with approval.
