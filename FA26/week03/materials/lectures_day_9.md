### Why this session exists

PS1 covers the perceptron and k-means, and neither was taught anywhere in weeks
1–3: "perceptron" appeared nowhere in the materials, and "K-Means" appeared once
as a word in a list of clustering algorithms on module_0 slide 5. Both are
foundational — the perceptron is the direct ancestor of Module 03, and k-means
is the opening of Module 02 — so they are taught here rather than dropped.
