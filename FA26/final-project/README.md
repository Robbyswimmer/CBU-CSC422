# Final Project — CSCI 4220, Fall 2026

**Everyone does the independent research project this year.** The Track 1 /
Track 2 choice from previous semesters is retired; the old Track 1 materials
are kept for reference under `FA25/retired-track1/`.

## What it is

A semester-long research project. Pick one of:

- **Replicate** a recent paper and analyse the result.
- **Extend** an existing method to explore a novel idea.
- **Tackle** a genuinely open-ended problem — unsolved RL tasks, robustness,
  bias and fairness, interpretability.

You may work **in pairs**, or in a small group with instructor approval.

## Why the proposal comes early

The proposal is your roadmap for the remaining ten weeks. Submitting it at the
end of week 2 means you spend the semester building rather than deciding — and
it gives me time to redirect a project that is too large, too small, or already
solved before you have sunk weeks into it.
